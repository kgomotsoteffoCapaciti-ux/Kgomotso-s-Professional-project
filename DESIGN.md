## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive, modern, and rigorously structured digital presence tailored for a high-potential professional in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, and Business Administration. 

The aesthetic merges **Corporate Modernism** with subtle **Glassmorphic Precision**—evoking systematic optimization, strategic command, and institutional credibility. It balances authoritative corporate gravitas with clean, approachable clarity. Visual density is purposeful, prioritizing clarity over ornamental filler.

### Emotional Response
- **Authority & Trust:** The visual weight of deep slate anchors professional credibility and operational capability.
- **Analytical Precision:** Crisp structural alignments, controlled typography, and refined data containers communicate efficiency and strategic problem-solving.
- **Modern Mobility:** Clean interactive micro-affordances signal a forward-looking mindset attuned to digital supply chains and business transformation.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around executive trust, corporate mobility, and algorithmic efficiency.

- **Primary (`#1E293B` / Slate 800):** Anchors headers, primary structural frames, key narrative text, and grounding interface modules. Expresses institutional stability and operational authority.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` / Logistics Blue):** Represents active operational flow, network connectivity, and executive action. Applied to primary calls-to-action (e.g., CV downloads, contact catalysts), key timeline links, and interactive focus states.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` / Supply Chain Emerald-Teal):** Symbolizes growth, resource optimization, cost efficiency, and verified status badges. Used sparingly for high-impact metric indicators, system health, and key operational milestones.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` / Midnight Slate):** Dedicated to high-contrast display typography, dark-mode base states, and high-impact hero framing.
- **Surface Foundations:** Background layers leverage cool slate tones (`#F8FAFC` canvas, `#FFFFFF` pure elevated cards, and `#F1F5F9` nested metadata containers) to maintain clean separation and readability without harsh chromatic vibration.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric, contemporary confidence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with the functional, neutral clarity of **Inter** for body text and data tables.

- **Headlines:** Use tightened negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) on Plus Jakarta Sans to project executive authority and precision.
- **Body:** Inter provides balanced vertical metrics, distinct glyph shapes, and high legibility across dense logistics manifests, project descriptions, and academic credentials.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Micro-labels utilize upper-case tracking with positive letter spacing (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) to establish visual separation for category tags, dates, and metric qualifiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The portfolio layout is organized on an asymmetric 12-column fixed-max grid (capped at `1280px` centered) to balance structured readability with dynamic editorial pacing.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` side padding. Experience timelines and project grids comfortably span asymmetric column pairs (e.g., 4 columns for sticky contextual metadata, 8 columns for narrative experience cards).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` side margins. Multi-column cards collapse to 2-column matrices.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` side margins. Timelines collapse into a linear single-spine layout with date stamps stacked directly above role titles.
- **Section Spacing:** Major executive narrative sections (About, Competencies, Experience, Projects, Contact) are separated by `space-2xl` to `space-3xl`, creating a deliberate, uncluttered reading rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp surfaces, low-contrast boundary lines, and controlled ambient diffusion rather than heavy dropshadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` providing a clean, non-distracting foundation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Solid `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-contrast shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Elevated Modules):** Translucent depth using `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Surfaces (Nav & Key Stat Cards):** 
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` in light context or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` in deep executive header blocks.
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(12px) saturate(160%)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` or `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The design uses **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry to maintain executive precision and corporate rigor:
- **Cards, Timeline Panels & Form Modules:** `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px). Keeps cards sharp, structured, and institutional without feeling harsh.
- **Buttons & Interactive Controls:** `0.375rem` (6px) default corner radius.
- **Technology & Status Pills:** `9999px` (Full Pill) for chips, tags, and category labels to clearly contrast against structured rectangular card layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CV Download / Contact Catalyst):** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `6px`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover shifts to `#1D4ED8` with a subtle elevation rise (`transform: translateY(-1px)`). Focus ring: `2px solid #3B82F6` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary Action (View Project / Credentials):** White background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC` background with `#0F172A` text.
- **Tertiary/Ghost (Inline Timeline Links):** Transparent background, text `#2563EB`, hover with text underline and `#EFF6FF` pill background.

### Metric & Glassmorphism Stat Cards
- Single-focus performance tiles displaying operational achievements (e.g., cost reduction %, ERP implementation lead-time, fleet optimization score).
- Built with Level 1 elevation, featuring an emerald-teal micro-bar (`3px` height, `#0D9488`) running along the top card edge.
- Numbers styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `font-weight: 700`, with `Inter` `label-md` uppercase descriptors beneath.

### Resume & Career Timeline
- Vertical track line: `2px solid #E2E8F0` positioned left-aligned on mobile and center-aligned or asymmetric-spine on desktop.
- Timeline Nodes: `12px` solid circular badge `#2563EB` with an outer white ring (`3px`) and subtle shadow. Current or active role node pulses with a `#0D9488` badge.
- Cards feature company/institution, location (e.g., Johannesburg, Gauteng), duration, key metrics achieved, and bulleted supply chain initiatives.

### Technology & Competency Pill Tags
- Background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#334155`, font-size `12px`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, shape `pill`.
- Categorized status: Supply Chain Tech (SAP, Oracle SCM, WMS) uses subtle `#EFF6FF` tint with `#1E40AF` text; Certifications (e.g., CIPS, APICS, Business Admin) display with `#F0FDFA` tint and `#0F766E` border accent.

### Contact & South African Regional Credential Card
- High-authority card containing professional headshot/avatar frame, direct business email, LinkedIn verification badge, and localized indicators (Gauteng / South Africa time zone indicator with live active status pill).
- Interactive copy-to-clipboard buttons for email and phone with instant checkmark feedback.
